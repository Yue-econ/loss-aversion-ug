In this part, you are randomly paired with another participant in this room. You will not learn who that person is, and they will not learn who you are.

One of you is randomly assigned to be Person A and the other Person B.

  • Person A receives $10 and decides how much of it to offer to Person B (any whole-dollar amount from $0 to $10).
  • Person B sees the offer and decides whether to Accept or Reject it.
  • If Person B accepts, Person B earns the offered amount and Person A keeps the rest.
  • If Person B rejects, both earn $0 in this part.

This part is played only once. You will learn the outcome of this part at the end of the experiment.
